Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

evolens (“we,” “us”) makes the evolens iPhone app and the evolens.app website. You can contact us at {{EMAIL}}.

evolens works without an account. Your card collection stays on your iPhone, and the app shows no ads. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and the choices and rights you have.

Information we collect and how we use it

On your iPhone only. Your collection stays on your iPhone and isn’t uploaded to us. That includes the cards you add, their details, and your collections and settings. Card recognition runs on your iPhone, and camera images aren’t sent to us. If you back up your iPhone, the backup may include this data under Apple’s terms.

To run the app.

  • Card prices: the app sends us the cards you view or own so we can return their market prices. We don’t keep a copy of your collection or a record of what an individual device looks up.
  • Security: the app uses Apple’s app-verification feature to confirm requests come from a genuine copy of evolens, which gives your installation a random identifier. Our systems also record technical information such as your IP address, an approximate location based on it, your app and iOS versions, and when requests happen. We use this to protect our services from abuse and keep them running.
  • Card images: images load from our servers and from third-party image hosts, which receive your IP address as part of any web request.

Purchases. Apple processes payments, and we never receive your payment details. Our subscription provider gives us a random customer identifier, your App Store purchase and subscription history, and basic device and app information. We use this to provide paid features, restore purchases and understand how subscriptions perform.

Product analytics. We collect information about how the app is used to help us improve evolens. This covers which features and screens are used, whether actions such as scanning and saving succeed, and how people respond to subscription offers. It comes with a random installation identifier, your app and iOS versions, your language, your App Store country and your subscription status. It doesn’t include your collection, photos, name, email or Apple ID. It isn’t linked to your purchase records or used for advertising. Our analytics provider is set not to keep IP addresses.

  • Where it’s on by default: only when the app determines from your device settings and App Store country that you’re in the United States. Everywhere else it’s off until you turn it on.
  • How to change it: go to Settings › Data & Storage › Share Usage Data in the app, at any time. Turning it off stops collection and removes the analytics data stored on your iPhone.

Support. If you contact us, we receive your email address, your message and any attachments. If you write through the app, we also receive basic app and device details. We use this to help you and keep a record of the conversation.

From Apple. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers, Apple gives us summarized usage and crash reports. Apple also reports totals of App Store visits and downloads that came from our marketing links.

Our website. evolens.app uses cookieless analytics to understand visits. It records the pages viewed, the referring link, your browser and device type, your language and your approximate location. IP addresses aren’t stored with this data. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, your visit isn’t counted. Our web host logs IP addresses for security.

How we share information

  • Service providers. These providers act on our behalf and handle information only to provide their services to us:
    • hosting and security;
    • content delivery;
    • subscription management;
    • product and website analytics;
    • email.
    Each provides the same or equal protection for that information as this policy.
  • Independent parties. Apple, third-party image hosts and the marketplaces you open from the app handle information under their own policies.
  • Legal and business reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it or to protect evolens and its users. Information may also be transferred as part of a sale or reorganization of the business.

We don’t sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. The app doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How long we keep information

  • Collection and settings on your iPhone: Until you delete them or the app
  • Security and diagnostic records: Generally no more than 30 days
  • Installation identifiers used for security: As long as needed to verify requests
  • Product analytics: Up to 1 year
  • Website analytics: Up to 6 months. Totals that don’t identify anyone may be kept longer.
  • Purchase records: As long as needed to provide subscriptions and meet legal obligations
  • Support messages: As long as needed to help you and keep a record

Your choices and rights

In the app:

  • turn camera access on or off in iOS Settings;
  • turn product analytics on or off in Settings › Data & Storage › Share Usage Data;
  • clear downloaded card pictures in the app’s Settings;
  • cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings (deleting the app doesn’t cancel it).

Your rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information;
  • object to or restrict how we use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • complain to a data protection authority.

To make a request, email {{EMAIL}}. Because evolens has no accounts, we may need some details from you to find your information. We won’t treat you differently for exercising your rights.

EEA and UK. We use your information on these legal grounds:

  • to provide the app and your subscription;
  • for our legitimate interests in security, reliability and improving evolens;
  • with your consent, when you turn on product analytics;
  • to meet legal obligations.

Information may be processed in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by our providers for these transfers.

Children

evolens isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

We use encrypted connections and collect only what we need. No system is completely secure.

Changes

We’ll post any changes here and update the effective date before they take effect in the app. If a change is significant, we’ll give more prominent notice.
