Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

evolens is an iPhone app for scanning, organizing and valuing your Pokémon TCG cards. You don’t need an account, your collection is kept on your iPhone, and the app has no ads. This policy explains what stays on your device, what leaves it, and why.

In this policy, “we” means evolens. You can reach us at support@evolens.app.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your collection: the cards and copies you add, their printing, condition, grade and certificate number, your collections, favorites and settings. These aren’t uploaded to us or synced to an account. If you use iCloud or computer backups, iOS may include them there.
  • The camera: evolens uses the camera only while you scan. Recognition happens on your iPhone, and video and scan images are never uploaded. During a scan session the app may keep small images of the cards it saw in temporary storage on your device. It deletes them when you save or discard the session.

What leaves your iPhone

Market prices

To show values, the app asks our pricing service about the cards you view or own. Each request identifies the card and printing, the condition being priced, and whether the card is in your collection. The service uses this to answer the request. Our pricing database doesn’t store a copy of your collection or record which cards your iPhone asked about. It keeps per-card information, such as when a card was last requested and whether anyone owns it, so that prices for those cards stay current.

Security and reliability

To confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of evolens, your iPhone uses an Apple security feature to create a random key. We store that key to verify requests and limit how many one installation can make. It isn’t your Apple ID, name or email, but it does identify your installation to our service.

When a new key is registered, we record your IP address to prevent abuse and delete that record after about 24 hours. Our hosting provider’s database recovery system may retain earlier copies of our data for up to 30 days. The provider also keeps diagnostic logs and traces for a sample of requests, currently for up to 7 days, which we use to keep the service running. They can include technical and network details such as the time, the result, your app and device software, and your approximate location (such as country and city), which is derived from your IP address.

Card images

Card pictures and set symbols are downloaded from our image service and from third-party card-image hosts. As with any web request, those services receive your IP address and which image was requested. Third-party hosts handle this under their own policies. Downloaded pictures are stored on your iPhone, and you can clear them in Settings.

evolens Pro subscriptions

Apple handles payment, and we don’t receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives a random customer ID created for your installation, your App Store purchase and subscription history, a device identifier Apple provides to app developers, and technical details such as your device model, iOS and app versions, App Store country and language. These connect your subscription to your installation. The app doesn’t give RevenueCat your name or email. This information is used to confirm your access to Pro, restore purchases, and give us subscription statistics.

Support

If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Contact Support in the app adds your evolens and iOS versions to the message. We use this to help you and to keep a record of the conversation.

Apple

Apple handles information under its own privacy policy when you use the App Store, make a purchase or use its security features. If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may give us aggregated usage and crash information.

Links and sharing

Marketplace links such as TCGplayer and eBay open in your browser, where those sites’ own policies apply. If you share a card, it goes wherever you choose to send it.

Our website

Our web host logs visitors’ IP addresses for security.

To understand how people find evolens and how our marketing performs, evolens.app uses an analytics service that doesn’t use cookies, doesn’t store anything on your device and doesn’t track you across other websites. For each visit it records the page, the site or link that brought you there (including any campaign tags in the link), whether you tapped an App Store button, your browser, operating system, device type, language and screen size, and your approximate location (such as country and city). Your IP address is used to work out that location and to count visitors, but it isn’t saved with the analytics. Visitors are counted with an identifier calculated from your IP address and browser details that changes every month, so visits can’t be linked over longer periods. We only look at combined reports. If your browser sends a Do Not Track signal, your visit isn’t counted.

App Store links on the website include a campaign label, such as which social platform your visit came from. Apple tells us how many App Store visits, downloads and purchases came from each label, as totals. The website doesn’t have access to your collection.

What the app doesn’t do

  • We don’t sell information collected through the app or share it for advertising.
  • The app doesn’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • The app has no advertising or usage-analytics tools.

Who receives information

Service providers that help us run evolens (hosting and security, website analytics, subscription management, and email) handle information on our behalf, and we expect them to protect it consistently with this policy. Apple and third-party image hosts act independently, as described above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect the service and its users, and it would move with evolens if ownership ever changed.

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where we and our providers operate. Where the law requires safeguards for those transfers, we rely on the data protection terms our providers offer.

How long information is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you remove it or delete the app. Scan images are kept until you save or discard the session.
  • IP records for new keys and diagnostic logs: as described above.
  • Security keys: for as long as the pricing service needs them to verify requests. We don’t currently delete them on a fixed schedule.
  • Subscription records: for as long as we use RevenueCat, unless deleted sooner on request, subject to its backup and legal-retention practices. Apple keeps its own records.
  • Website analytics: up to 6 months. We may keep totals that don’t identify anyone for longer.
  • Support email: for as long as needed to help you and keep a reasonable record of the conversation.

Deleting the app removes your collection and the app’s data from your iPhone. iOS may keep a small technical credential that contains nothing about you or your cards. Existing backups and the records listed above aren’t affected.

Security

The app connects to our services over encrypted connections, and we try to hold only what’s described here. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t guarantee absolute security.

Children

evolens isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we’ll delete what we can identify.

Your choices and rights

  • You can turn off camera access in Settings › evolens. Without it, you can’t scan.
  • You can clear downloaded card pictures in the app’s Settings.
  • You can cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Deleting the app doesn’t cancel it.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict how it’s used, and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, email support@evolens.app. Because evolens has no accounts, we hold very little that identifies you and may need details from you to find it. If we can’t match a request to any information, we’ll tell you.

Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on providing the app and your subscription (contract), on our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and running and in understanding how people find evolens, and on legal obligations where they apply.

Changes

If the app’s practices change, we’ll update this page and its effective date before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@evolens.app.
