Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

evolens is an iPhone app for keeping track of your Pokémon TCG cards. You don’t create an account, and the app has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. Your collection stays on your iPhone. This policy explains what the app keeps on your device, what it sends and to whom, and why.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your collection: the cards and copies you add, their printing, condition, grade and certificate number, your collections, favorites, and settings. These are stored only on your device. We can’t see them, and they aren’t synced to our servers. iOS includes them in your iPhone backups.
  • The camera: evolens uses the camera only to recognize cards while you scan. Video is processed on your iPhone and is never uploaded. While a scan is under way, the app may keep small images of the cards it saw in its own temporary storage on your device. It deletes them when the scan session ends.

Deleting the app deletes all of this from your iPhone.

What leaves your iPhone

Market prices

To show card values, the app asks our pricing service (run by evolens on Cloudflare) for the cards you view or own. Each request includes the card and printing identifiers, the condition being priced, and whether that card is in your collection. We use that last part only to keep prices fresh for cards people own, and we store it per card, not per person.

Requests are verified with Apple’s App Attest. Your iPhone creates a random key for this; it isn’t your Apple ID, name or email. Our service stores that key so it can check requests and limit their rate. To prevent abuse, it also records your IP address when your iPhone registers a new key, to limit how often that can happen, and deletes that record within 24 hours.

Cloudflare keeps diagnostic logs of a sample of requests for up to 7 days, which we use to keep the service running. They can include technical details such as the time, the address requested on our service, the result, your app and device software, and your approximate location (such as country and city), which is derived from your IP address. We don’t use any of this to identify you. Scanner images are never sent to this service, and it doesn’t keep a copy of your collection.

Card images

Card pictures and set symbols are downloaded from the evolens image server (on Cloudflare) and from public card-image hosts (TCGdex, pokemontcg.io and Scrydex). Like any web request, those servers receive your IP address and which image was requested.

evolens Pro subscriptions

Apple handles payment for subscriptions. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. RevenueCat receives an anonymous ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription history from the App Store, and technical details such as your device model, iOS version, app version, App Store country, preferred language and Apple’s per-app device identifier (IDFV). RevenueCat uses these only to check whether you have Pro and to restore your purchases.

Links to other sites

Links to marketplaces such as TCGplayer and eBay open in your browser. Those sites’ own privacy policies apply there.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell your information or share it for advertising.
  • We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We don’t use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

evolens isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • You can turn off camera access at any time in Settings › evolens. Without it, you can’t scan.
  • You can cancel a subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.
  • You can delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone.
  • For any question, or a request about information held by our pricing service or by RevenueCat, email support@evolens.app.

Changes

If the app’s practices change, we’ll update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
