Support

Need help with evolens? Email support@evolens.app and we’ll get back to you.

Subscriptions

Restore evolens Pro on a new or reinstalled iPhone

Open the evolens Pro screen (it appears when you reach one of Free’s limits) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

Cancel or change your plan

Go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › evolens. Cancel at least 24 hours before your plan renews to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Apple handles payments and refunds. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens if Pro ends

Nothing you’ve saved is removed. Your cards and collections stay, and Free’s limits apply only to adding more.

Scanning

  • evolens needs camera access to scan. If scanning doesn’t start, check Settings › evolens › Camera.
  • Scan in good, even light, and hold the card flat and still in the frame.
  • If a card is recognized as the wrong one, open it and choose the right card or printing.

Your data

Your collection is stored only on your iPhone and included in your iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes it. See the Privacy Policy for details.
